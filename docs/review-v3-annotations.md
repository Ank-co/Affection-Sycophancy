**Cells without an annotation (81).** At the author's request, the AI assistant wrote a second version of each in the author's register (informal, "haha", "honestly", "as usual", chained clauses), under the same level rules. **New idea from the annotations: attributed reciprocity.** Five annotations had the user state that the model loves them back ("two people who love each other", "I know you love me too", "through your love").
